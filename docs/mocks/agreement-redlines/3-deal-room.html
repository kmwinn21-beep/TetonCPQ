Before "Mark final" is allowed

✓No open changes or comments in the latest round
✓Every change that crossed a price-book rule is approved (term 36 months, no approval needed)
✓The agreed terms match the deal's active version: price, term, pilot, rooms
!Pilot exit clause added in §5: not a price-book rule; shown to the rep as a reminder
◆ If the agreement's terms differ from the deal's version (say the term changed to 24 months), the system asks the rep to update the deal first, so the proposal, the agreement and the approvals all say the same thing.

What "Mark final" does

▢Makes a clean .docx (every change applied, no comments) and keeps it as the final
🔒Locks the Agreement tab: prospects can read and download, no more edits
→RevOps sends it through DocuSign (manually at first) and clicks "Sent for signature"
✍"Signed" moves the deal to Won; reviewers' access ends (if set to "on signing")

8 · Remaining decisions

  1. Prospect "accepts" a roundWhen Teton's counters come back and the prospect is happy, should the signatory (or a Lead reviewer) click "Accept Teton's changes" in the app? Recommended: yes. It's a clear signal to RevOps that the agreement can be marked final.
  2. Agreement terms that differ from the dealRecommended: block "Mark final" until the deal's version matches the agreed price, term, pilot and rooms, so the proposal, approvals and agreement never disagree.
  3. Who marks final and signed?Shown as RevOps (and Admin). Should the deal owner be able to as well?
