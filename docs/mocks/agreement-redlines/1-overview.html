Redlining the agreement in the deal room

RevOps uploads the Word draft to the deal room. The prospect's team can suggest edits in the browser, or keep working in Word and upload their redlined file. Teton accepts, rejects or counters each change, with internal notes the prospect never sees, and downloads a real Word file with tracked changes at every round.

Yes

It's doable, with one honest limit. We read the .docx (including any tracked changes and comments already in it), show it as a page in the deal room, and record every edit as a suggestion. At any round we write it back out as a .docx with real Word tracked changes, so RevOps and outside counsel keep working in Word. The limit: complex Word formatting (multi-level auto-numbering, text boxes, fields, unusual headers and footers) may not look exactly like Word in the browser. The Word file stays the official copy, and a typical agreement (clauses, numbered sections, a pricing table) works well.

  • Recommendation: start with D plus reading tracked changes (upload any round, see its changes and comments in the deal room, accept or reject, Teton-only notes, download). That covers counsel who stay in Word and gets the review flow working. Then add in-browser suggesting (A) as the second step.
  • If browser editing has to look exactly like Word from day one, B is the shortcut, and worth pricing out before we commit.
  • This plugs into the agreement tracker in the deal flow mock (backlog #3): rounds are the tracker's steps, and a §-change that crosses a price-book rule raises an approval.

6 · Decisions

  1. Who can see the Agreement tab?Recommended: only people on the share link that the rep marks as "can review the agreement" (signer and counsel), not everyone on the deal room.
  2. Who can accept changes on Teton's side?Recommended: rep for wording and payment terms; anything that crosses a price-book rule needs its approver (ties to the approval workflow).
  3. Is the deal room the official record, or the Word file?Recommended: the Word file RevOps downloads is official; the deal room is where the back-and-forth happens.
  4. Phase 1 scope: upload rounds with tracked changes read in (D+), or wait for browser editing (A)? Recommended: D+ first.
