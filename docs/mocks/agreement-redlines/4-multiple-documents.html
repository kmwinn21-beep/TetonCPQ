Every deal starts with two documents, MSA and Order Form. Each one has its own rounds, its own "whose turn", and is marked final on its own. The rep can rename, add or skip documents. The deal is signed once every document it needs is final.

6 · What changes under the hood

Moves to each document

Rounds, changes, decisions, notes, whose turn, "Accept Teton's changes", Mark final / Reopen, the final file, the version-match check.

Stays on the deal

People and levels, tab access, invite settings, permissions, Mark signed, the activity feed (now tagged by document).

Existing V1 data

Any agreement already started becomes the deal's MSA, with an empty Order Form added beside it. (Or "Agreement" if you'd rather not guess.)

Size

Medium. The Word engine doesn't change; the work is the data layer, the switcher in both screens, and signed waiting on all documents.
