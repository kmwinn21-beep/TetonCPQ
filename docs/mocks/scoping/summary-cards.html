Questions

1. "Compute units" or "Edge units"?Everywhere else (bottom bar, table column, proposal) says Edge units. Using one name avoids "are these different things?"
Recommendation: pick one and use it everywhere. If customers know them as compute units, I'd rename Edge units to compute units across the app and proposal; otherwise keep "Edge units" on the card.
2. Count the whole community, or just this plan?The table's totals are for room types across the community's plans ("All plans"). A community with two floor plans would show the community totals on both summaries.
Recommendation: the community totals (what gets priced), matching the table's Total row, with "This plan: 40 rooms" under the compute unit number when the community has more than one plan.
3. In the downloaded PDF too?The Summary tab is drawn from the download, so the cards come along.
Recommendation: yes, the same cards on the download's summary page.

Small fix I'd make at the same time: "1 sensors" in the "Rooms set individually" list should read "1 sensor" (visible in your screenshot).
